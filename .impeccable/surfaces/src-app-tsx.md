Scope: whole app shell and every page (Operate mode). Mobile-first PWA used one-handed in a home gym.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Munich '72 Program. Training, eating and body are three disciplines, each with its own flat Aicher colour, and every movement pattern has a geometric pictogram. Refuses the category default: near-black UI, one blue accent, rounded glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Light ground (daylight garage, phone on a bench) with a dark variant for night sessions. Flat discipline colours: training blue, nutrition green, body orange; silver hairlines, deep blue-black ink. No red, no black, no gradients, no glass. Archivo grotesk; condensed width for numerals. Hairline-ruled modules instead of padded cards, 4px grid, 12px radius max.

STORY: A lifter opens the app, sees today's session and macros at a glance, starts logging, and taps through sets in seconds; progress reads honestly on fixed scales.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Coloured discipline header band naming the section. Today: next session as pictogram rows (pattern pictogram, exercise, sets x reps, weight in condensed numerals), primary Start button full width at thumb height; nutrition module with ruled macro bars below. Bottom tab bar, active tab filled with its discipline colour.

FORM: Munich '72 Olympic identity (Otl Aicher), my ranked #1 of 7; seed key dd9c2d9b. Signature move: geometric movement pictograms + discipline colour coding. Raises kept: fixed chart scales, fixed set columns, stamped set states, hairline modules.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
